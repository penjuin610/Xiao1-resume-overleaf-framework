# Local Setup

This repository is intentionally public-safe.

To actually use it for a personal resume workflow, create a **local-only** companion folder that is not committed.

## Recommended local structure

```text
resume-overleaf-framework/
├── SKILL.md
├── references/
│   ├── private-config-template.md
│   └── resume-facts-template.md
└── private/
    ├── private-config.local.md
    └── resume-facts.local.md
```

## What goes in the private files

### `private-config.local.md`

Put here:

- your Overleaf project URL
- your preferred PDF naming convention
- your browser/login assumptions
- any local workflow notes

### `resume-facts.local.md`

Put here:

- your actual name and contact details
- your real schools, dates, titles, and locations
- your hard truth constraints
- your core-vs-optional experience rules
- your style and compression preferences

## Privacy rule

If a file contains any of the following, keep it private:

- name
- email
- phone
- address or city
- employer history
- resume bullets
- private URLs
- Overleaf links
- browser/session assumptions tied to one person
